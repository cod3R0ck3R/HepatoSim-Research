# Sato et al. 2023: candidate data source, not a validation result

Reviewed 2026-10-22 against the article XML retrieved from Europe PMC. DOI: [10.1016/j.mbplus.2022.100127](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mbplus.2022.100127); PMID 36632559; PMCID PMC9826883. Article title: *Fibrosis resolution in the mouse liver: Role of Mmp12 and potential role of calpain 1/2*.

The experiment uses male C57Bl/6J mice, four weeks of CCl4 injury, and terminal sampling 1, 3, 7, 14 and 28 days after cessation. These are sacrificed-animal groups, not repeated longitudinal observations of the same mice. Figure legends report n=4–6; the exact animal-level allocation must be obtained before analysis. The paper's use of repeated-measures ANOVA needs clarification against that design; do not adopt it automatically.

Sirius-red histology is measured as labelled area divided by total field area, using five non-overlapping fields per section. Figure 3 includes its image-analysis comparison and an MMP12-inhibition condition. Figure 1 also reports Col1a1 transcript expression; transcript abundance is not collagen mass. Figure 2 contains multiphoton imaging. The precise units and normalization of each plotted panel must be read from the original figure before any extraction. Fields are technical subsamples, not independent animals.

The data-availability statement says data are available on request. The XML names supplementary DOCX and PPTX files, but these have not yet been retrieved or assessed. No raw numerical collagen dataset has been obtained. No graph values or individual measurements have been invented or digitized.

This is a promising withdrawal-phase source, but it does not by itself supply a measured injury-phase trajectory. It concerns toxicant injury in mice, not alcohol-associated human cirrhosis. Mapping a model collagen state to stained tissue area, matching injury timing, and distinguishing elastin remodelling from collagen changes require a new prospective observation model and benchmark specification.

The checked Bougueon2024 XML contains no reference to this DOI, PMID, or Arteel; its Sato reference is a different 2021 HSP47 study. Absence from the reference list does not prove independence from model development. Author confirmation and a complete data-role audit remain necessary.

Next discriminating step: ask the corresponding research group for deidentified animal-level Sirius-red measurements, units/normalization, group and animal identifiers, sampling days, technical-field aggregation, missing/excluded observations and access/reuse terms. Confirm whether the dataset was used in Bouguéon model development. Request no human health data. A specific draft should reference this paper rather than claim a validated model.

Local retrieval provenance records the download URL, SHA-256 and time. The article states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Keep the source XML and supplements out of the public project pending rights review; publish this original factual summary and source link only. This note reports source triage, not calibration, independent biological prediction, human transferability or clinical validation.
