# SEMA4D–PLXNB2 — targeted update, 24 October 2026

Research mechanism, not an established liver treatment. [Full bilingual dossier and sources](../../Docs/23-SEMAIndependent.md).

- Preclinical scope: TAA mouse injury-phase anti-SEMA4D experiment and separate Sema4D-knockout/AOX1 findings. No human cirrhosis efficacy or reversal claim.
- New external human evidence: GSE135251, 206 NAFLD biopsies; SEMA4D increased with advanced fibrosis, PLXNB2 did not meet the positive-association criterion. No alcohol-related or decompensated cohort inference.
- Model representation: ligand abundance, receptor engagement and cell-specific signaling are distinct. No defensible quantitative mapping to Kappa rates or a drug efficacy percentage has been established.
- Alternatives: PLXNB1/CD72 signaling, SEMA4C–PLXNB2 interaction, immune-cell composition and systemic AOX1/retinoid effects. RNA association does not determine these mechanisms.
- Safety/clinical status: no clinical benefit/safety or regulatory approval assessment performed in this milestone; no change to existing dossier classifications, no dose recommendation.
- Next discriminating evidence: independent donor-level spatial/cell-type analysis, followed by receptor-selective loss/rescue and appropriate collagen/matrix and viability controls. Persistent response after PLXNB2 loss would oppose an exclusive B2 mechanism.
